== Description ==

WooCommerce AI Product Advisor reviews your catalog, surfaces the products with the highest improvement potential, and proposes targeted edits to them — ready to apply with a single click.

**Why use it**

* **Skip the blank page.** Get concrete, on-brand suggestions instead of staring at an empty description field.
* **Focus on what moves the needle.** Products are ranked by optimization potential, so you spend time where it actually pays off.
* **Stay in control.** Every suggestion is a diff you can edit, accept, or reject — nothing changes on your store until you say so.

**Features**

* AI-generated suggestions for products, grounded in your store's brand tone.
* Brand voice onboarding so suggestions sound like you, not like a generic model.
* One-click apply with a side-by-side diff viewer and inline editing before you commit.
* History and undo — every applied change is logged and can be reverted.

== Installation ==

1. Download the latest version from the releases page.
2. Go to **Plugins > Add New** in your WordPress admin.
3. Click **Upload Plugin**, select the downloaded zip file, and click **Install Now**.
4. Activate **WooCommerce AI Product Advisor** from **Plugins > Installed Plugins**.
5. Open **Product Advisor** (it appears right under **Products** in the admin menu) and complete the onboarding flow.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= What are the requirements? =

You need WordPress 6.8 or later, WooCommerce 10.5 or later, and PHP 7.4 or later.

= Does this plugin require a Jetpack connection? =

Yes. WooCommerce AI Product Advisor uses a WordPress.com / Jetpack connection to communicate with the AI service. You will need a free WordPress.com account to complete the connection during onboarding.

= Will this plugin change my products automatically? =

No. Every suggestion is presented as a diff that you can review, edit, accept, or reject. Nothing is applied to your store until you explicitly confirm the change.

= Can I undo changes made by the plugin? =

Yes. Every applied change is logged in the suggestion history and can be reverted with a single click.
